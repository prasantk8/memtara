# Week 0 checklist — before the first call is dialled

Everything here must be true before contact #1 goes out. A missed item on
this list is not a paperwork gap — it is either a wasted first impression
(you don't get a second one with a compliance officer) or a compliance
exposure that gets worse with volume. Work top to bottom; several items
block later ones.

Owner key: **F** = founder, **L** = external UAE-qualified legal/compliance
adviser, **B** = whoever the founder reports to (the board, per the
90-day-plan framing).

---

## Sourcing and list

- [ ] **F** — First 15–20 qualified contacts sourced and logged in
      `marketing/LEAD_LIST.md`'s tracking table, following the procedure in
      `TARGET_LIST_CRITERIA.md`. You do not need all 60 before starting —
      you need enough to run the first week without stalling, and real
      conversion data from the first calls should inform how you source the
      rest.
- [ ] **F** — Firm-type mix in that first batch weighted toward DIFC/ADGM
      independents and private banks, per `TARGET_LIST_CRITERIA.md`'s
      priority order — not toward whichever firms were easiest to find.
- [ ] **F** — Every logged contact has a `Source` and a retrieval date filled
      in, per `marketing/LEAD_LIST.md`'s own rule. No name without a source.
      No exceptions for "I'm pretty sure I remember where I found this."

## Outreach and compliance

- [ ] **L** — The outreach process in `OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md` reviewed by a
      UAE-qualified adviser before any message is sent at volume, per
      `marketing/LEAD_LIST.md` §6. This is not optional because the
      messages say "research" instead of "sales" — the same data-protection
      and identity/opt-out rules apply either way, and that document says so
      explicitly.
- [ ] **F** — A real reply address that a human actually checks daily is
      live before the first message sends. Silence on a reply is worse than
      a slow one.
- [ ] **F** — Opt-out handling process decided: where a "no thanks" gets
      logged, and a personal commitment to never re-approach through a
      different channel after one. Write this down now, not in the moment
      you get the first opt-out.
- [ ] **F** — Both outreach variants (`OUTREACH_SEQUENCE.md` Variant A and
      B) drafted with real, checkable specifics filled in — not left as
      placeholder brackets. Variant A's enforcement-action citations
      double-checked against the primary source, per `incident_source.md`'s
      own caveat that it is a secondary source.

## Interview mechanics

- [ ] **F** — `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` read enough times that Q0 through the
      closing line can be run from memory. Do not read questions off a
      screen on a call with a compliance officer — it signals a script, not
      a conversation, and undercuts rule 3 (they are the expert, not the
      audience).
- [ ] **F** — A note-taking template ready that maps directly to
      `SCORING_RUBRIC.md`'s fields, so scoring happens live or within the
      hour, not reconstructed from memory two days later.
- [ ] **F** — Decide and rehearse the exact answer to "what do you do?" if
      asked before the closing question — the guide's suggested line
      ("I'll tell you in ten minutes — I want your unfiltered experience
      first") needs to sound natural, not evasive, which takes a few
      run-throughs out loud.
- [ ] **F** — If calls will be recorded (even just for the founder's own
      note-taking), consent language decided and ready to say at the start
      of the call, and checked against the same data-protection frameworks
      `marketing/LEAD_LIST.md` §6 names. Recording someone without telling
      them is a worse first impression than any script error.
- [ ] **F** — Calendar has real, protected 25-30 minute slots blocked across
      the coming weeks — not squeezed between other commitments. A rushed
      discovery call produces rushed, low-quality answers.

## Scoring and decision infrastructure

- [ ] **F** — `SCORING_RUBRIC.md`'s spreadsheet columns built and ready
      *before* call 1, not after call 5. Scoring retroactively means the
      first few calls get reconstructed from memory, which is exactly the
      failure mode the rubric exists to prevent.
- [ ] **F + B** — The pre-committed re-open trigger (2 of the first 5 calls
      naming a funded alternative priority, per `SCORING_RUBRIC.md`) is
      explicitly acknowledged by whoever the founder reports to, before
      calls start — not discovered as a surprise if it fires. If the
      trigger fires, the founder should not be the sole judge of whether to
      honour it; that is exactly the 11pm-rationalisation risk the trigger
      is designed against.
- [ ] **B** — Decision bands in `SCORING_RUBRIC.md` ("proceed unchanged" /
      "proceed re-scoped" / "no-go") reviewed and accepted by the board
      contact before evidence exists — the whole point is that they cannot
      be renegotiated once the data is in.

## Logistics

- [ ] **F** — A simple way to confirm and reschedule calls (calendar link or
      manual back-and-forth — either is fine, but decide now) so a booked
      call doesn't quietly slip.
- [ ] **F** — A short, honest answer ready for "who else have you talked
      to?" or "is anyone else doing this?" — likely: nobody yet, this is
      the first round. Say it plainly if asked; per
      `marketing/LEAD_LIST.md:24`, dodging this question reads worse than
      the honest answer.
- [ ] **F** — Confirm the interview guide, buyer map, and outreach sequence
      never imply the product makes the firm compliant, and do not claim
      the product proves a control "operated" or that governance was
      "effective" — reread the closing line in `INTERVIEW_GUIDE.md` once
      more immediately before call 1, since that is the one moment product
      language is allowed to appear at all.

---

Once every **F** item above is checked, dial the first call. The **L** and
**B** items should be moving in parallel — do not let legal review become a
silent blocker with no deadline; ask for a turnaround date when you send it.
