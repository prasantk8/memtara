Prove structured-product suitability, on this device

What stays on this device, always:

  • Your income, liquid assets, risk tolerance and existing holdings — the four figures in your vault file.
  • Your signing key.

What this page sends to the bank, and nothing more:

  • A cryptographic proof that those figures clear the product's published thresholds.
  • One bit: suitable or not suitable.
  • The public terms of the assessment (the product, the thresholds, the time window) — the bank already knows these; they are not secret.

The proof is built and generated inside this browser tab. Your vault file is read from disk and never uploaded; nothing about your finances is transmitted, logged, or stored by this page.

This page's text is English only. It has not yet been translated for French- or Arabic-reading clients — see clients/web-prover/SPIKE.md for why this stage stopped short of that and what a translated version would need.
